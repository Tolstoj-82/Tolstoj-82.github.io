About the comparison

Lines show the approximate Hard Drop reference: L 10.7%; J, I, Z 13.7%; O, S, T 16.1%.

Colour shifts from red toward green as expected sampling error shrinks (roughly 1/√N). It describes sample maturity, not proof that the RNG matches.

In the ideal uniform-candidate model, 95% overlap takes about 320 pieces on average; around 800 gives about a 95% chance at that sample size. These are simulation estimates. Results fluctuate; passing once does not mean staying there.

The ROM selector uses timer readings, so actual long-run frequencies depend on timing and may differ from this reference.
