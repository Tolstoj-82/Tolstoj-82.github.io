About the game

Chris & Triss is a Tetromino-based puzzle and block-stacking game for the original Game Boy, created by Tolstoj for EuroChrisKA. Version 1.1 fixes bugs that were present in version 1.0 and adds new features.

Similar to the most popular block stacker, it provides features for training and competition, including extended levels, detailed results, custom music, and tools for controlled piece sequences.

Features

  • Endless and Digout modes with separate rules and presentation.
  • Extended levels from 0 to 9 and A to M, including heart levels.
  • Six-digit SPS (same piece set) seeds.
  • Line caps, Master Mode, Zen Mode, and a hidden-score option.
  • Piece distribution tracking and detailed post-game results.
  • Three-entry detailed leaderboards with mode, level, line, and SPS information.
  • Original music and sound effects written by Tolstoj.
  • Custom endings with conditional messages.

Version 1.1 bug fixes

  • Corrected game-over messages, celebrations, and score-dependent endings.
  • Fixed menu navigation, level display, and leaderboard data.
  • Repaired Hide Score, line-cap endings, and Digout presentation.
  • Expanded the SPS randomizer to six digits.
  • Moved the font one pixel lower for better readability.
  • Fixed music and sound-effect interference, allowing non-intrusive sound effects while the music plays.
  • Removed residual and corrupted tiles during screen transitions.
  • Added name entry.

Future tasks

  • SRAM storage for the name and leaderboard.
  • Two-player mode.

Stay tuned for these updates.
