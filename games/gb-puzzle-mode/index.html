About the ROM patch

GB Puzzle Mode, created by DLB, turns Game Boy Tetris into a series of placement puzzles. Each puzzle starts with a prepared stack and gives you two pieces. Your job is to find the best way to fit them into the board.

The game judges the final arrangement against a stored solution. Find the ideal placement for full points, or an accepted second-best placement for half points. Anything else counts as a miss—even if the pieces fit without topping out.

Features

  • 8,461 distinct two-piece puzzles on prepared boards. Version 2.6 contains no duplicate entries when comparing the starting board and supplied piece pair.
  • Ideal and second-best solutions with full or half credit.
  • Selectable starting levels to change the pace.
  • Repeat, skip, and reveal controls for practice.
  • Saved scores and a six-character player name.
  • 107 result-message entries, including community references and jokes.
  • Score-dependent endings, including a surprise for a very low score.

How to play

  1. Enable Puzzle on the title screen and choose your music setting.
  2. Choose a starting level, then begin. Move and rotate the two pieces into position.
  3. After both pieces lock, read the result. Try again, continue to another puzzle, or reveal the ideal placement.

Tip

Use B to repeat a puzzle before revealing its answer. After the result, Select displays the ideal placement so you can compare it with your attempt.

What counts as a win?

After both pieces have been placed, the game compares every square of the 10-column, 18-row playfield with its stored solution. Only whether a square is occupied matters. The whole arrangement must match; simply filling a gap or avoiding a top-out is not enough.

ResultConditionCredit
IdealThe board exactly matches the best solution.Full points
Second bestThe board matches the alternate solution.Half points
MissThe board matches neither solution.Penalty

The game remembers previous results for each puzzle, so repeating the same result is not an unlimited source of points.

Hidden endings: what triggers the rockets?

Hold A + B + Start + Select together in Puzzle Mode. The game saves your score and chooses an ending from your current total. Reaching a threshold by itself does not start the animation.

ScoreEnding text
−50,000 or lowerLET ME RATE YOUR PERFORMANCE THIS GAME
100,000 or higherYOU PRETTY GOOD AT TETRIS
−49,999 to 99,999SCORE SAVED / PLAY SOME MORE / IF YOU LIKE THIS

Yes, the low-score threshold is negative. The game has a separate ending for a sufficiently large losing score. “YOU WIN” in a puzzle result is one of the ordinary success messages, not a special trigger for the ending.
